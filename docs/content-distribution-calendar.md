# 30-Day Organic Content Distribution Calendar (Phase 23)

## Operational Guidelines for Business Owner
* **Strictly Non-Automated**: All posts, messages, comments, and outreach must be manually written and posted by a human operator.
* **80/20 Value Ratio**: 80% of activity must provide immediate educational value; 20% should introduce relevant tools/resources.
* **Zero PII**: Never store or post personal information of community members or outreach contacts.
