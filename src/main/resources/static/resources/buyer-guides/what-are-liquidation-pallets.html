What Are Liquidation Pallets? A Buyer's Essential Guide

📅 Updated September 2026 ⏱️ 6 Min Read ✍️ Simpatico Logistics Team

A liquidation pallet is a wooden or plastic shipping pallet loaded with bulk merchandise that major retailers, distributors, or manufacturers clear out at steep discounts to make room for new inventory. These pallets allow small businesses, e-commerce sellers, flea market vendors, and discount store operators to source goods at pennies on the dollar relative to original retail pricing.

1. Primary Types of Liquidation Inventory

Not all liquidation merchandise is created equal. Understanding the inventory condition is critical to projecting your profit margins and labor requirements:

  • Overstock & Surplus: Brand-new, untouched inventory that never reached retail shelves due to overproduction or warehouse clearance. Overstock items feature pristine retail packaging and zero customer usage.
  • Shelf-Pulls: Items that were displayed in retail stores but removed to make space for seasonal floor updates. Packaging may feature price stickers, security tags, or minor shelf wear, but the merchandise remains unused.
  • Customer Returns: Products returned by online or in-store buyers. Condition ranges widely from unopened returns and buyer remorse to items with damaged packaging, missing accessories, or functional defects.
  • Refurbished / Tested Working: Electronics or appliances that were inspected, repaired, tested, and repackaged for secondary distribution.
💡 Pro Tip for New Buyers

If you are launching your first reselling operation with limited refurbishing capability, target Overstock or Shelf-Pull pallets. While customer return pallets offer higher theoretical profit margins, they require testing, sorting, and grading labor.

2. Pallet Dimensions & Packaging Standards

In North America, standard wholesale liquidation pallets utilize a 48" x 40" GMA wooden footprint. Depending on the product category, stacked pallet heights typically range between 4 feet and 7 feet tall, shrink-wrapped securely for LTL freight shipment.

Product density varies significantly by merchandise category:

  • Apparel & Softlines: High piece counts (200–500 units per pallet), packed tightly in Gaylord boxes.
  • Consumer Electronics: Moderate piece counts (30–100 units), packaged in individual manufacturer boxes.
  • Small Domestic Appliances: Lower piece counts (15–40 units), bulkier packaging requiring full pallet volume.

3. How Liquidation Pallets Are Priced

Liquidation inventory is traditionally priced as a percentage of Estimated Retail Value (MSRP) or as a flat rate per pallet:

  • Percentage of MSRP: Unmanifested or manifested lots are offered at 10% to 25% of original retail value depending on category and condition.
  • Flat Rate Pallet Pricing: Standardized mystery or category pallets (e.g., General Merchandise) sold at fixed price points ($300 to $900 per pallet).

4. Key Considerations & Risk Management

To operate a sustainable reselling business, buyers must factor in the full landed cost of each purchase—which includes freight shipping, liftgate fees, warehouse storage, and expected shrink (unsellable items).

Before purchasing, always request an itemized manifest document whenever available to audit expected SKU counts and retail values.
