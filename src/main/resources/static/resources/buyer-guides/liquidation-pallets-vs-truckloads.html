Liquidation Pallets vs. Truckloads: Which Is Right for You?

📅 Updated September 2026 ⏱️ 7 Min Read ✍️ Simpatico Logistics Desk

When sourcing wholesale liquidation inventory, one of the most consequential decisions you will make is whether to purchase individual LTL (Less-Than-Truckload) pallets or commit to a full 53-foot truckload (FTL). Freight expenses can account for 20% to 50% of your total landed cost, making order volume a critical lever for overall profitability.

In This Comparison:
  • 1. Single Pallets (LTL Shipment)
  • 2. Full Truckloads (53ft FTL Shipment)
  • 3. Freight Economics & Cost-Per-Unit Impact
  • 4. Direct Feature Comparison
  • 5. How to Choose for Your Business

1. Single Pallets (LTL Shipment)

LTL pallet orders (1 to 4 pallets) are ideal for growing resellers, bin stores with limited footprint, or businesses testing a new merchandise category.

Advantages of Single Pallets:

  • Lower Initial Capital Outlay: Orders range from $300 to $2,500 depending on category, preserving cash flow.
  • Reduced Storage Requirement: Fits easily into small retail backrooms, garages, or standard storage units.
  • Flexibility: Test multiple product categories (e.g., small appliances vs. apparel) without committing to high volume.

Drawbacks: Higher freight cost per unit, liftgate surcharges ($50–$100), and longer shipping transit times through regional freight hubs.

2. Full Truckloads (53ft FTL Shipment)

A standard 53-foot dry van truckload holds 24 to 26 tall pallets (or up to 52 double-stacked pallets for light merchandise). Buying at the truckload scale connects buyers directly with major retail distribution centers.

Advantages of Full Truckloads:

  • Dramatically Lower Freight Cost Per Unit: Dedicated FTL rates reduce shipping costs per item by 40% to 60% compared to LTL rates.
  • Direct Warehouse Shipping: Shipped point-to-point from retail distribution centers with minimal handling and faster delivery.
  • Maximum Profit Margins: Preferred tier pricing lowers wholesale cost per unit significantly.
🚚 Freight Math Example

Shipping a single LTL pallet 800 miles might cost $250 ($0.50/unit for 500 items). Shipping a full 26-pallet truckload (13,000 items) the same distance might cost $1,800 total—reducing your freight cost to just $0.13 per unit.
