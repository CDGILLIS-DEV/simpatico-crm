Liquidation Sourcing Guide for Online, Storefront & Bin Store Resellers

📅 Updated September 2026 ⏱️ 7 Min Read ✍️ Simpatico Reselling Advisory Team

Successful liquidation buyers customize their inventory strategy based on their specific distribution model. A product lot that fails on Amazon FBA might generate exceptional returns in a local discount bin store or on eBay. This guide maps wholesale liquidation categories to specific reselling business models.

Reselling Models Covered:
  • 1. Discount Bin Stores ($1 - $10 Fixed Price Days)
  • 2. Amazon FBA & E-Commerce Merchants
  • 3. eBay, Poshmark & Mercari Sellers
  • 4. Flea Market Vendors & Local Liquidators
  • 5. Summary Matrix & Inventory Request

1. Discount Bin Stores ($1 - $10 Fixed Price Days)

Discount bin stores rely on high item counts, small product footprints, and rapid inventory velocity. Customers hunt through unmanifested bins filled with mixed general merchandise on tiered price schedules (e.g., $10 Friday down to $1 Wednesday).

  • Best Inventory Category: Small General Merchandise, High-Count Customer Returns, Accessories, Toys, Small Electronics.
  • Preferred Shipment Format: Full 53ft Truckloads (24–26 Gaylord Pallets) containing 10,000 to 25,000 individual items per truckload.

2. Amazon FBA & E-Commerce Merchants

Amazon and Walmart Marketplace enforce strict product condition policies. E-commerce merchants must prioritize brand compliance and box integrity.

  • Best Inventory Category: Overstock, Factory Case-Pack Overstock, Sealed Shelf Pulls.
  • Key Sourcing Requirement: Intact original retail packaging with scannable UPC/EAN barcodes. Avoid unmanifested customer return lots unless you possess dedicated refurbishing workflows.
💡 E-Commerce Tip

When sourcing for Amazon or eBay, request manifested lots so you can calculate your estimated ROI per SKU using current marketplace sell-through rates prior to purchasing.

3. eBay, Poshmark & Mercari Sellers

Multi-channel marketplace sellers excel at handling individual SKU listings, minor open-box items, or tested refurbished goods.

  • Best Inventory Category: Consumer Electronics, Designer Apparel & Shoes, Small Home Appliances, Power Tools.
  • Preferred Shipment Format: 1 to 4 LTL Pallets focusing on high-value single items ($30+ MSRP per unit).

4. Flea Market Vendors & Local Liquidators

Flea market vendors require low landed unit costs ($0.50 to $3.00) to maintain attractive cash pricing for in-person shoppers.

  • Best Inventory Category: Apparel Gaylords, Cleaning Supplies, HBA (Health & Beauty Care), Tools, Bedding & Home Textiles.
