Frequently Asked Questions

What types of inventory lots do you match?

We source overstock, customer returns, shelf-pulls, and factory-refurbished merchandise across Electronics, Apparel, Home Goods, and Hardware.

What is the typical matching timeframe?

Our automated matching engine evaluates submission parameters immediately. Vetted matches are typically presented within 24 to 48 hours.

Are there minimum purchase requirements?

Minimums depend on supplier pallet sizes. Most inventory packages range from single-pallet lots ($1,000+) to full truckload manifests ($50,000+).
